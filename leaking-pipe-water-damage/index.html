Leaking Pipe Water Damage Help in San Bernardino

Most pipe leaks start without any warning. The drip is hidden in a wall cavity, a sink cabinet or beneath the slab, so the first clue tends to be a stain, a spongy patch of floor or an oddly high water bill. By then the materials around the pipe have often been wet for weeks. We serve San Bernardino homeowners by tracing the full wet area and drying every bit of it, including what is out of sight.

The plumbing repair stops the water, but it does not remove what has already soaked in. Getting it out is the work we do. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the wet materials, open only what has to be opened, and set air movers and dehumidifiers until readings are back to normal. Mold often gets a head start during a long leak, which is why we look for it and treat the surfaces involved.

Right Now

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off the WaterWhen a toilet or sink line is dripping, shut the valve on the supply right at that fixture. If you cannot find the source, turn off the main shutoff valve for the house.
  2. Keep Power AwayTurn off the breaker serving the area when water is close to a light fixture, switch or outlet. Leave any wet cord or electrical device alone.
  3. Check the Water MeterMake sure nothing in the house is using water and read the meter. Movement on the dial means water is escaping out of sight, perhaps beneath the slab.
  4. Empty and PhotographBefore a repair is made or anything goes in the trash, remove what is stored near the leak and get pictures of the pipe, the staining and the warping.
  5. Have the Area MeteredBefore the opening is sealed, get the surrounding materials checked with a moisture meter. Inside the cavity, moisture can remain even when the wall surface feels dry.
The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • Fasteners from remodeling work or wall hangings that nick or pierce a line inside the wall.
  • A P-trap or drain connection under a sink that loosens and drips each time the basin empties.
  • A compression fitting or supply line connection at a sink or toilet that has loosened or worn with use.
  • Soil movement and rubbing that wear a hole in a line buried below the slab.
  • Pipes in exterior walls or crawl spaces that crack after a freeze and seep once they thaw.
What Is at Risk

What the Water Damages

Ruined Cabinet Bases

Particleboard cabinet bases under a sink swell and crumble once they absorb water. They do not return to shape, so long leaks often mean replacing the base.

Mold Inside the Wall

Mold thrives in a wall cavity that is both damp and dark. It can spread across the hidden face of the drywall long before anything appears on the room side, which is common when a leak is slow.

Rotted Framing

Studs and the plate beneath them soften and decay when they sit wet for weeks. Once framing lumber is no longer sound, it must be removed and new wood put in.

Flooring Damage

Moisture from a leaking slab or wall line spreads out below the finished floor. Laminate seams swell, hardwood boards cup, and the glue holding tile or vinyl releases.

Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Find Every Wet Area

Using a thermal camera and moisture meters, our crew checks cabinets, floors and walls and marks each wet spot, even those far from the pipe that water reached by running along the framing.

2

Open What Is Needed

To get at wet cavities we open only specific spots, take out unsalvageable cabinet parts, drywall or insulation, and seal anything moldy in bags so its spores stay out of the rest of the home.

3

Structural Drying

Drying equipment stays in place, typically for 3 to 5 days, until moisture in the framing and subfloor is back to normal. Readings are taken at each visit, and the setup is changed as the materials dry.

4

Treat and Document

We apply antimicrobial treatment to affected surfaces and give you moisture logs and photos. Those records show the area was dry before repairs and help with any claim.

Coverage

What About Insurance?

A slow leak and a sudden one are not handled the same way by insurance. Policies often cover damage that is sudden and accidental. A leak lasting weeks or months usually falls outside that, and certain policies name it as an exclusion. A hidden leak you had no way of knowing about may be handled more favorably than one that was visible and ignored. Your agent and the policy itself are the places to confirm. So the adjuster can work from solid facts, we provide photos, moisture readings and notes on the leak source.

Cost

What Affects the Cost

  • How long the pipe leaked and how far the moisture spread from it.
  • Which finishes and flooring got wet, as some can be saved by drying and some cannot.
  • Mold, if there is any, because containment and removal come first and drying finishes after.
  • How many rooms or levels the water reached, including a ceiling below the leak.
