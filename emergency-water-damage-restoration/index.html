How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Going away longer than a few days? Close the main water valve first.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.

Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in San Bernardino

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration: What Homeowners Ask

We do, every night of the year, weekends and holidays included. Expect questions about the water source and safety hazards, followed by first steps for you and a crew being scheduled. Arrival time depends on distance, weather and how many calls are active, and we will give you an honest estimate on the phone.
It is an emergency when the water has not stopped, is close to outlets or wiring, contains sewage, drips from a ceiling, or has saturated drywall, carpet or wood floors. If a little water lands on tile and you can dry it completely, there is no rush. Not sure? Pick up the phone. Our team is happy to say it can wait instead of leaving you to guess.
If the main valve is safe to reach, close it. At the panel, switch off circuits serving wet areas, and stay out of standing water while doing so. Get electronics, area rugs and smaller valuables into a dry room. Take photos for your insurer. Keep pets and children clear, and never wade into possible sewage.
Plan on air movers and dehumidifiers running 3 to 5 days in most cases. Our team comes back to take moisture readings, repositioning or pulling machines as each area dries. Bigger jobs and dense materials like plaster or hardwood may need extra time. The equipment is loud and warm, but it needs to run continuously to do its job.
If water has already soaked floors, walls or ceilings, call a restoration team first. We can shut off or isolate the supply on arrival and begin removing water right away. A plumber is still needed for the permanent pipe repair, which can usually happen during drying. Standing water does more damage each hour, so do not let it sit until the repair.
