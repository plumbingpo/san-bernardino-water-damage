Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

When a pipe bursts in San Bernardino or the surrounding area, San Bernardino Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.
